# Language and RTL

## Sub-features
English and Arabic; Arabic sets `dir=rtl` on the document.

## How to get to it (user POV)
Settings, General, Language.

## Driving it with drive.mjs
`drive.mjs <port> lang ar`, capture, `drive.mjs <port> lang en`.

## Gotchas
Errors made in the main process may stay English; report that as a finding.
