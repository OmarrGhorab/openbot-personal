### Added

- Egyptian Arabic interface. Select العربية (مصر) in Settings > App language, or keep System default on an iPhone that uses Arabic. The right-to-left layout takes effect the next time you open the app.

### Changed

- The app has two interface languages: English and Egyptian Arabic. The German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Turkish and Simplified Chinese interfaces are removed. A saved choice of one of these reads as System default.
