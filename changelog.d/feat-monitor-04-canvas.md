- **Monitor canvas:** A Monitor channel now opens on a canvas. It shows one card per agent, in columns by hand-off order, and a line between agents that work together. An active line is blue and dashed; a finished line is grey. Each line shows its message count or the hand-off. Click a card to select the agent, and click a line label to open the conversation. The arrow keys move between cards and Enter selects one. Cards keep their place when the channel changes.
